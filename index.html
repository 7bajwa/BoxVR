How to play
  • Quest 3: open this page in the Meta Quest Browser, tap ENTER VR. Use hand-tracking (make fists) or Touch controllers.
  • Punch each glowing target with the matching hand as it reaches the gate. Straights go forward, hooks sweep sideways (follow the chevrons), uppercuts drive upward.
  • Score = timing accuracy + punch power, multiplied by your streak. Misses and wrong-hand punches break the chain.
  • Browser: keys S D F (left: upper, hook, jab) and J K L (right: cross, hook, upper). Space starts/stops. On phones tap the pads.
